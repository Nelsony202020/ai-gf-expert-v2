### 3.4 Things that look like mistakes

1. **`315:2731` Prompt Structure renders an empty trailing chip.** `Item 4` in
   `Article / Prompt Structure` (`308:4251`) has no Show toggle — only items 5–8 do. The author
   blanked item 4 with a single space instead, so the instance draws `Pose → Outfit → Scene →`
   followed by a bare 20px pill. Confirmed visually. Build three chips; and the component
   should gain a `Show item 4` boolean.
2. **`315:2701` Comparison A/B has no verdict.** The component description says "Two options
   side by side with a clear verdict". This instance hides the Verdict entirely, so it is a
   bare two-panel block. Either supply a verdict or use a different component.
3. **`315:2602` Prompt Comparison has no conclusion.** Same pattern — the component spec calls
   for "+ one-line conclusion"; the `Conclusion` layer is off.
4. **Verdict labels are inconsistent between instances.** `315:3021` uses `My take`,
   `315:3080` uses `Bottom line`. Pick one convention, or at least be deliberate: `My take`
   inside a Comparison A/B duplicates the kicker used by the four standalone
   `Article / My Take` blocks.
5. **`Article / Important` used as a status note.** `315:2655` overrides the label to
   `Still testing`. The component is documented as "A restrained warning about a mistake that
   costs coins or ruins results" — a testing-status disclaimer is not that. Consider a
   dedicated note component.
6. **`Article / Reference Cheat Sheet` used against its stated purpose.** It is documented for
   "terms/keywords (camera & pose terms, hair lengths, style names)" and explicitly says
   "DO NOT USE: … explanatory sentences". `315:2822` fills it with diagnostic sentences
   (`the model misunderstood part of your prompt`). It also renders 3 groups in a 2-column
   wrap, so group 3 sits alone on row 2 with dead space to its right — a 2×2 or 4-group
   layout would read better.
7. **Near-duplicate prompt strings across three blocks.** The beach/black-dress/sunset prompt
   appears four times in slightly different wording:
   - `315:2602` side A: `She is sitting at a beach wearing a black dress while the sun is setting behind her.`
   - `315:2602` side B: `sitting, beach, black dress, sunset, warm lighting, front view`
   - `315:2638`: `She is sitting at a beach during sunset wearing a black dress and looking toward the camera.`
   - `315:2785`: `sitting on the beach, black dress, sunset, looking at camera, warm lighting`
   - `315:2949`: `woman sitting on the beach, black dress, sunset, looking at camera, warm lighting, medium shot`
   Intentional as a running example, but the `at a beach` / `on the beach` and
   `front view` / `medium shot` drift is worth a copy pass.
8. **Related Guides rows 1 and 3 are near-duplicates**: `OurDream AI Image Prompt Guide` and
   `OurDream AI Prompt Guide`, both in `Prompts & Characters`. Likely one is the wrong target.
9. **Apostrophes are consistently curly (U+2019)** everywhere they appear
   (`don’t`, `isn’t`, `wouldn’t`, `doesn’t`, `that’s`, `Don’t`). No straight `'` found in any
   string in this article. Keep them curly on build.
10. **Image grid column widths do not tile the 740 column.** The 2-up grids use 300 + 16 + 300
    = 616px and the 3-up grid uses 236×3 + 16×2 = 740px. The 2-up grids are 124px narrower than
    the text column and are left-aligned, so they read as misaligned against every other block.
    Also, the two figures in `315:2580` have different heights (451 vs 448), so their captions
    do not sit on the same baseline; `315:2893` is correctly matched at 557/557.
11. **Nothing is drawn wider than 740.** Every direct child measures 740 or less; the widest
    non-740 blocks are the two 616px image grids noted above.
